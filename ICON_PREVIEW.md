## Choose Your Adventure restyle

The selection screen now has a compact player header, a two-line title with the companion map artwork, nine progress segments for the nine solo levels, and three full-width mode cards. Each card uses its established mode color, a short description and an action footer. A Start here / Keep going / Next challenge badge points toward the next available stage. Locked cards preview the mode and state the exact prerequisite progress. Progress counts unique completed level IDs through the existing won-level set; Battle wins do not increase solo progress. Existing mode unlock and navigation rules are unchanged.

Design rationale: NN/g's [teen usability research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/usability-of-websites-for-teenagers/) supports short text, clear visual hierarchy, fast access to goals and an age-respectful tone. Its [recognition guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/) supports keeping mode differences and unlock conditions visible. [CAST's UDL guidelines](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) support meaningful choice, clear goals and challenge with support. These findings inform the design; the screen has not yet been tested with Grade 7 students.
